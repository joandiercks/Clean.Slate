About Clean Slate

Turning neglected properties into places people can feel proud of.

Clean Slate is a volunteer-powered organization that brings local residents together to clean, beautify, and care for neglected residential and commercial properties.

01

What We Do

We organize volunteers to create visible, lasting improvements throughout the community.

01

Property Cleanups

We clean up neglected commercial and residential properties through organized volunteer efforts. Projects may include weed removal, litter pickup, trimming, raking, debris removal, and general exterior beautification.

02

Ongoing Maintenance

When needed and resources are available, we offer maintenance support for previously cleaned properties to help preserve their appearance and support long-term upkeep.

Our Mission

We create visible change by organizing volunteers to remove weeds, litter, debris, and other signs of neglect at no cost to property owners.

By bringing people together around tangible projects, we help create cleaner, more welcoming places while strengthening community pride and connection.

02

Who We Are

Clean Slate is powered by people who care about the places where they live, work, and spend time.

Our organization is made up of volunteers from the local community. People of all experience levels are welcome, whether they want to participate in a single cleanup, join recurring projects, contribute specialized skills, or help support events behind the scenes.

JD

Founder

Joan Diercks

Clean Slate was founded in 2026 by Joan Diercks, who leads and organizes the organization’s volunteer projects, partnerships, and community efforts.
